Frequently asked questions

Everything people usually ask before adopting their first Peeg.

Is Peeg free?

Yes. Peeg can be downloaded and played for free. The app includes optional purchases, like coin packs or removing ads, but they aren't required to enjoy the game.

Which devices does it support?

Peeg is available on iPhone, iPad, and Android devices. You can download it from the App Store or Google Play using the links on this page.

What age rating does the app have?

Peeg has an age rating of 9 and up.

What languages is it available in?

The app is available in English and Spanish. You can switch languages from the settings inside the app.

Can I play with my friends?

Yes. Every Peeg has a unique code you can share to add friends, send them gifts, and see them on the Peeg feed.

How often is it updated?

The team ships new items, decorations, and mini-games every month, along with ongoing fixes and improvements.
